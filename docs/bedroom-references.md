# A bedroom around 1999

The room is a nostalgic composite, with objects from the late 1980s and 1990s that could have accumulated in a teenager's room by 1999. It is an original illustration, rather than a recreation of a particular person's bedroom.

Period references:

- [The Strong: Tamagotchi](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/tamagotchi/)
- [V&A: popular toys through the decades](https://www.vam.ac.uk/articles/the-most-popular-toys-ever-a-guide-through-the-decades)
- [The Strong: Pokémon Red and Green](https://www.museumofplay.org/games/pokemon-red-and-green/)
- [Smithsonian: Game Boy](https://americanhistory.si.edu/collections/object/nmah_1253117)
- [Sony company history: Walkman](https://www.sony.com/en/SonyInfo/CorporateInfo/History/sonyhistory-e.html)

Music and television use official YouTube uploads with visible player controls. Playback availability depends on YouTube, the uploader, region, and browser autoplay rules. Files are not downloaded, rehosted, or extracted as audio. A direct YouTube link remains available for each selection.

Embed references:

- [YouTube embed and privacy-enhanced mode](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780?hl=en)
- [Required minimum functionality](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/required-minimum-functionality)
- [Developer policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies)

The room artwork was generated with the built-in imagegen tool; its brief is in [bedroom-art-brief.txt](bedroom-art-brief.txt). Interactive collection artwork and room effects are original SVG/CSS. This is an unofficial fan project.
# Live radio alternatives

The Walkman links to [SomaFM Underground 80s](https://somafm.com/u80s/) (early UK synthpop and new wave) and [90s90s Radio](https://www.90s90s.de/) (1990s stations). These open their official sites in a separate tab. No streams are embedded, extracted or rehosted.
